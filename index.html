Research

Current work

Audiovisual 40 Hz ASSR + SSVEP entrainment

Five-condition study (visual-only, auditory and audiovisual stimuli with carrier and clicker variants) of gamma-band entrainment, testing whether in-phase audiovisual stimulation increases 40 Hz SNR. Wrote the SOPs and the analysis pipeline (Welch PSD, SNR, Morlet ITPC).

BioSemiPsychoPyLSLMNE-Python

LanguageEEG — syntax and comprehension ERPs

ERP study of N400, P600 and LAN/ELAN components with 32-channel EEG and eye tracking. Nine conditions crossing attention mode and grammaticality; integrated a Tobii Pro Nano eye tracker with custom calibration.

EEGERPTobiiPsychoPy

Multimodal cognitive assessment from facial behavior

Multimodal deep-learning model that predicts MoCA sub-domain scores from facial video recorded during a verbal learning task. Contributed to model development, ablation studies, GPU training setup and manuscript drafting.

Earlier research

Development and validation of a novel 32-channel rodent EEG system

Master's thesis · Jul 2018 – Nov 2020 · Best Thesis Award
  • Designed custom electrodes, performed impedance correction, recorded ERPs and analyzed data in rodent models.
  • Found distinct ERP patterns for passive versus reward-modulated visual and auditory stimuli.
  • Tracked arousal-dependent oscillatory changes continuously throughout recordings.
  • Maintained low, stable impedance for more than 6 months of behavioral recording.

Effect of oscillatory activity on axon regeneration of retinal ganglion cells in rd1 mice

Undergraduate research · Feb 2016 – Jul 2018 · Advisor: Dr. Chuan-Chin Chiao
  • Showed that electrical oscillations enhance axonal regeneration in cultured retinal tissue.
  • Validated ganglion cell electrical activity with multielectrode arrays (MEA) under pharmacological modulation.
